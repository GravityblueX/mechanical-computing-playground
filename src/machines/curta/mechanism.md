# Curta — mechanism boundary

US 2,525,352 describes a compact circular arrangement of result- and revolution-counting members around a common driving member. The Curta.org operator transcription documents controls and invariants from the user's perspective.

The current UI does **not** reproduce patent pinions, axles, tooth groups, detents, clearing linkage, carriage lock, or production Type I/II geometry. Its arrows and cylinder are **P** teaching elements. See [`research/curta-source-map.md`](../../../research/curta-source-map.md) for figure anchors and uncertainties.
