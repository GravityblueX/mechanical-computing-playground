# Curta — documented operation roles

The Curta.org operator transcription supports, at its stated provenance precision:

- setting decimal values with knobs;
- clockwise full turns completed at the home/zero detent;
- additive and subtractive handle positions;
- result/product and counter/quotient readouts;
- decimal-place changes through carriage position;
- repeated-turn multiplication;
- division procedures using carriage, counting, overshoot and an undoing turn;
- zero-stop requirements for carriage and other controls.

These are **H** operator-procedure claims, not permission to infer internal geometry. The current `314 × 27` Curta page is a **P** operation summary. Full provenance and limits: [`research/curta-source-map.md`](../../../research/curta-source-map.md).
