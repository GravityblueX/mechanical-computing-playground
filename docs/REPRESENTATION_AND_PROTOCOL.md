## Reading rule

- **H/R columns** say only what the cited source chain supports.
- **P/M columns** identify repository decisions judged by deterministic state/events and tests.
- A browser “step” can mean an operator action for a discrete calculator, but for the continuous lesson it means **pause the explanation and inspect a relation**. It is not a historical physical tick.
- `open / unverified` is a result, not an invitation to fill gaps with smooth-looking gears.
