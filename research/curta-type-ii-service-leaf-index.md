# Curta Type II service-leaf and revision index

**Checked: 2026-09-02**

## Scope and method

This is a page/leaf provenance instrument, not a production history or repair guide. The three Type II service targets were resolved from the actual `href` values in the existing `mycurta.com` index; the `vcalc.org` page is a mirror of that access layer. All three PDFs are image-only. Every page of the English-green and German-1967 scans was rendered for visual inspection; the German-green cover/front matter was inspected to establish its corresponding German notice, but its remaining leaves were not independently indexed. OCR/vision was used only to navigate and read visible text. Ambiguous codes remain `unreadable`.

The index/filename is access provenance only. Manufacturer identity, model, date and revision claims below come from document-internal text.

## Cross-scan comparison and chronology conclusion

1. The green scans visibly share stage letters and corresponding language structure; the dated German issue visibly includes N/O/P/Q/R and later V/VI/X/Y/E leaves. Exact correspondence cannot be claimed merely from stage letters because the printed coding schemes and pagination differ.
2. No exact green-to-1967 leaf pair was established as textually/visually identical at available resolution. Similar responsibility names do not prove unchanged leaves or parts.
3. The German document itself establishes one issue date: **autumn 1967**. Neither green document exposes a frozen issue date.
4. No replacement sequence can be ordered across the green scans from printed dates. The green front matter proves that replacement leaves/latest modifications existed and were marked in red, but readable per-leaf dates/revision codes were not found. Right-edge marks on pp. 27, 31 and 33 are unreadable.
5. Therefore the defensible result is: **one separately dated 1967 German service issue plus two undated 43-page green replacement-leaf assemblies; no complete replacement-leaf chronology.** PDF order, collector filenames, modern `Dec.2013` wrappers/marks, and separate 1951–1953 BOM/drawing-sheet dates do not supply that missing chronology.

## Evidence boundary

Directly inspected manufacturer-origin page/leaf text is **H/E1 at the exact precision above**. Comparison of scan organization is H/R where interpretation is required. Nothing here establishes production launch, serial adoption, interchangeability, hidden geometry, linkage timing, force, tolerances, wear, safe speed, or repository P/M event identity.
