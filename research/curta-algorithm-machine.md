# Curta as a mechanical algorithm machine

Curta is best explained by its operation decomposition: setting registers encode operands, a crank performs repeated transfer, a carriage position changes place value, and counters expose repetitions/results. Calling it merely a strange electronic-calculator predecessor hides the human-executed algorithm and its mechanical constraints. The teaching files under `src/machines/curta` intentionally remain a grade-D operational model rather than a full emulator.
