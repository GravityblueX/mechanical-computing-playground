# Stepped drum and pinwheel

Leibniz-style stepped drums expose a selected number of teeth along the drum; Odhner-style pinwheels expose a selected number of pins. Both use repeated crank motion and carriage shifts to express place value. This project presents both as conceptual models (grade D), not geometric reconstructions. The explanatory increment is the shared operation trace: effective engagement, crank count, shift, and accumulation are inspectable as separate facts.

Sources to consult for historical precision include original manuals/patents, museum reconstructions, and specialist Curta/arithmometer documentation. No claim here depends on a modern CPU analogy.
