# Mechanical learning prior art

This project is a counterfactual teaching machine. Li & Mao (2024) reported all-mechanical neural-network work with in-situ backpropagation/adjoint fields; that is a **REAL MECHANICAL LEARNING RESULT**, not an invention of this repository. Their physical substrate and field interactions differ from the browser model.

The tested Stage A/B cores are a **PEDAGOGICAL TRANSLATION**: explicit numerical phases and events make gradients inspectable. Hand cranks, signed gradient shafts, and reduction gears are **COUNTERFACTUAL MACHINE ELEMENTS**. Physical parameter storage, calibration, friction, noise, and update accuracy remain **OPEN ENGINEERING QUESTIONS**. This repository makes no originality claim for mechanical backpropagation.
